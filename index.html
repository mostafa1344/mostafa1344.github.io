🎮 What is laya-vs-jev?

laya-vs-jev is an exciting desktop application that pits two different AI systems against each other in the classic Chrome T-Rex runner game. One AI runs locally using MLX (Apple's machine learning framework), while the other uses a hosted AI service. Watch them compete side-by-side in real-time, complete with live metrics showing which AI is performing better!

Whether you're curious about AI technology, love gaming, or just want to see something cool, this app gives you a front-row seat to an intelligent race. The best part? You can record replays of each race to compare strategies afterward.

🔍 How It Works

The T-Rex runner game (the one you play when your internet is down in Chrome) runs as the competition arena. Both AI systems receive the same game state and must decide when to jump or duck. The application tracks their performance in real-time and displays:

  • 🏆 Score: How far each AI progresses
  • ⏱️ Reaction Time: How quickly they respond to obstacles
  • 🧠 Decision Accuracy: Percentage of correct choices
  • ⚡ Speed: How many actions per second each AI takes

🛠️ Quick Troubleshooting

Even the best apps occasionally have hiccups. Here are quick fixes for common issues:

  • App won't start: Make sure you have at least 4GB of free RAM and close other resource-heavy programs.
  • Slow performance: Close unnecessary background apps. The game needs about 2GB of free memory to run smoothly.
  • Connection errors with hosted AI: The hosted AI needs an internet connection. Check your Wi-Fi or Ethernet cable.
  • Replay doesn't save: Ensure you have at least 500MB of free disk space. Replays are saved automatically to your Videos or Downloads folder.
